Questions

Good to know

What do you need from me?

Four things: this sign-up, the onboarding form, your approval of the messages, and one 5-minute login. After launch, keep an eye on your LinkedIn and Sales Navigator inboxes — that's where people reply to you.

How soon does it start?

Your messages and lists are ready to review within 48 hours of your onboarding form and first payment. Outreach starts once you approve them and we've logged in.

Do I need LinkedIn Sales Navigator?

Most of the outreach runs in Sales Navigator, and the onboarding form asks whether yours is active. Without it, LinkedIn allows shorter connection notes, and we write to that limit.

Is it safe for my account?

Every message is sent by hand by a person, with no bots or automation tools on your account, and we keep each account under daily sending limits.

How does the login work? Do you keep my password?

You fill in a short private page (your LinkedIn email, the city you log in from, a couple of good times) and share your password with your outreach specialist through a secure link. We never store it. When your specialist logs in, LinkedIn sends you a code and you pass it on by WhatsApp, the private page or email.

Will the messages sound like me?

They're written in first person from your own answers, in the tone you pick. You see both versions first and can ask for changes.

How does billing work?

A monthly subscription through Stripe at $1,000 a month, plus any sales tax. Stripe emails your receipt.
